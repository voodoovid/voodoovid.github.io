Mind The Gap

Rosie, the vintage red tube train, is due out on the morning service, but the depot sidings are jammed with tube trains and engineering locos.

Shunt them along their tracks (trains only go forwards and backwards) until Rosie can run into the tunnel on the right.

How to play

The goal

Rosie, the vintage all-red tube train, is stuck in a jammed depot. Clear her track so she can run into the tunnel on the right of her row, under the road. The signal by the tunnel turns green once nothing stands in her way; then drive her out and the level is won.

Moving the trains

Drag any train with your finger or the mouse. Trains only run forwards and backwards along their own track, never sideways, and they can't pass through each other or off the edge of the yard.

  • Single cars and yellow battery locos (the engineering engines) take up two squares.
  • Two-car trains take up three squares, so they're harder to get out of the way.

Tapping a train picks it (a gold outline shows which one). That matters for the keyboard and controller.

Moves and stars

One move is sliding one train, however far it goes. Sliding the same train again right away still counts as that one move. Best beside your moves is the fewest moves the level can be solved in.

  • ★★★ Solve it in the fewest moves.
  • ★★ Use up to half as many moves again.
  • ★ Use more than that, or use a hint.

Your best stars for each level are saved on this device.

The buttons

  • Undo takes back your last move. Keep tapping to go further back.
  • Restart puts the level back how it started.
  • Hint makes the next best move for you (the train flashes white). A level you use a hint on earns only one star.
  • Levels lets you pick any level. Every level is open from the start.
  • ? opens this guide. 3D / 2D switches the view. ♪ turns the music on or off, and 🔊 turns all sound on or off.

The levels

There are 40 levels in four fare zones of ten. They get harder the closer you get to the centre of London: Zone 4, Zone 3, Zone 2 and the busiest, Zone 1, from a few moves to almost forty. Play on the title card starts the first level you haven't solved yet.

Tips

  • Work backwards. Find what blocks Rosie's track, then what blocks that train, and so on.
  • Two-car trains running up and down across Rosie's row are usually the real problem. They need room above or below to get clear.
  • Sometimes a train has to move the "wrong" way first to make room for another.
  • Stuck? Undo a few moves rather than restarting, or take one hint.
