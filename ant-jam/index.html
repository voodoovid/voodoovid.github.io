Ant Jam

Rosie, the red ant, has found a sugar crumb for the queen, but the tunnel is jammed with ants and beetles.

Slide them along their lines (bugs only walk forwards and backwards) until Rosie can carry her crumb out of the exit on the right.

Drag to slide. Keys: Tab picks a bug · arrows slide it (or pick the next one across) · Z undo · R restart · H hint · L levels · V 3D/2D · ? guide

Crumb delivered!

How to play

The goal

Rosie, the red ant with the sugar crumb and the pink bow, is stuck in a jammed tunnel. Clear a path so she can walk out of the exit on the right of her row (the gold arrow). When nothing stands between her and the exit, slide her out and the level is won.

Moving the bugs

Drag any bug with your finger or the mouse. Bugs only walk forwards and backwards along the way they face, never sideways, and they can't walk through each other or the tunnel walls.

  • Ants take up two squares.
  • Beetles take up three squares, so they're harder to get out of the way.

Tapping a bug picks it (a gold outline shows which one). That matters for the keyboard and controller.

Moves and stars

One move is sliding one bug, however far it goes. Sliding the same bug again right away still counts as that one move. Best beside your moves is the fewest moves the level can be solved in.

  • ★★★ Solve it in the fewest moves.
  • ★★ Use up to half as many moves again.
  • ★ Use more than that, or use a hint.

Your best stars for each level are saved on this device.

The buttons

  • Undo takes back your last move. Keep tapping to go further back.
  • Restart puts the level back how it started.
  • Hint makes the next best move for you (the bug flashes white). A level you use a hint on earns only one star.
  • Levels lets you pick any level. Every level is open from the start.
  • ? opens this guide. 3D / 2D switches the view. ♪ turns the music on or off, and 🔊 turns all sound on or off.

The levels

There are 40 levels in four groups of ten: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced and Expert. They get harder as you go, from a few moves to almost forty. Play on the title card starts the first level you haven't solved yet.

Tips

  • Work backwards. Find what blocks Rosie's path, then what blocks that bug, and so on.
  • Beetles standing up and down in Rosie's row are usually the real problem. They need room above or below to get clear.
  • Sometimes a bug has to move the "wrong" way first to make room for another.
  • Stuck? Undo a few moves rather than restarting, or take one hint.
