Flip Five

Guess 1 of 6

How to play

Find the hidden five-letter word in six guesses. Every guess has to be a real word. After each one the flaps turn over to show how close you were.

RGreen: the letter is in the word and in the right spot.
AAmber: the letter is in the word, but in another spot.
TDark: the letter is not in the word.

A letter can appear more than once. If you guess two Es and only one turns green or amber, the word has just one E.

Green letters you have found are filled into the next row for you, outlined in green, and typing skips over them. You can switch this off in Settings.

Must keep greens

Switch this on for a harder game. Every green letter must stay in its spot in every later guess. Amber letters can be left out.

Words

On a keyboard: type letters, Enter to guess, Backspace to erase. After a game, Enter starts the next word.
