How to play

Find the hidden five-letter word in six guesses. Every guess has to be a real word. After each one the flaps turn over to show how close you were.

RGreen: the letter is in the word and in the right spot.
AAmber: the letter is in the word, but in another spot.
TDark: the letter is not in the word.

A letter can appear more than once. If you guess two Es and only one turns green or amber, the word has just one E.

With Must keep greens on, green letters you have found are filled into the next row for you, outlined in green. If you type that same letter it goes in its spot; any other letter jumps ahead to the next open spot.

Tap any flap in the row you're typing to put the next letter there. It's outlined in amber; tap it again to let go. On a keyboard the arrow keys do the same. Green letters are locked and can't be changed.

When a word ends, Today's Wordle plays the day's New York Times Wordle. It shows until you've played today's puzzle to the end, then comes back with the next one.

Settings > Appearance switches between a light and a dark board, or follows your device.

The music plays a random track, and a different random one with each new word; pick a track in Settings. Solve the word for a cheer (bigger the fewer guesses you needed), miss it for a raspberry.

Must keep greens

Every green letter stays in its spot in every later guess, filled in for you. A letter you know is in the word but that has only one spot left it could go (every other spot is green, or you already tried it there) is filled into that spot too, outlined in green. Amber letters can be left out.

Words

On a keyboard: type letters, Enter to guess, Backspace to erase. After a game, Enter starts the next word.
