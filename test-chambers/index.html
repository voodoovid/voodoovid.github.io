How to play

The goal

Reach the green EXIT of each chamber. Your portal gun fires two portals, blue and orange, onto white panels only. Step into one and you come out of the other. Speed and direction carry through, so whatever you fall into, you leave at the same speed. Dark panels refuse portals.

Touch · iPad / iPhone

  • Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left half to walk.
  • Right thumb: drag anywhere on the right half to look around.
  • BLUE / ORANGE: fire that portal at the crosshair.
  • JUMP. GRAB: pick up or put down a cube or a sentry.

Trackpad & keyboard

  • Click once and the pointer locks to the centre, under the gun's sights. Then slide on the trackpad to look around.
  • Two-finger swipe up / down to walk forward / back; sideways to sidestep.
  • Click: blue portal. Two-finger click (or right click): orange.
  • WASD or arrows walk · Space jump · E grab
  • Q blue · X orange · R restart · H this guide · T ask for a hint · Esc pause · F full screen

Controller

  • Left stick walk · right stick look
  • LT / LB blue · RT / RB orange
  • A jump · X grab (cube or sentry) · Y restart
  • Start pause · Select full button list

What you will meet

  • Acid: do not touch. SaDos finds this funny.
  • Cubes sit on the round buttons to open doors. Cubes cannot pass a reset field.
  • Sentries see in front of them and shoot after a moment's warning. A live one jams portals inside its red ring. Come at one from behind or the side and GRAB picks it up: it sleeps while carried, shields you from other sentries, weighs down a button, and wakes a moment after you put it down, facing the way you were. It cannot be pushed over: only a falling cube, or another sentry's fire, puts one out for good.
  • Sentries were never told about portals: if one sees another sentry through a pair, they shoot each other.
  • Purple gravity panels: step on one and everything loose in its zone falls up (not you). Step on it again to put things back.
  • Amber-ringed buttons are on a timer: they hold their door for a few seconds after you step off.
  • Coolant is as deadly as acid, but things float in it.
  • Moving panels slide along a lit wall. A portal placed on one rides with it, so pick your moment to step through.
  • Turning panels spin half a turn every few seconds. A portal on one goes round to the other side with it.
  • Office windows are tougher than they look, but not bullet-proof.
  • Light walkways are solid: walk on them. Carrier streams lift whatever steps in and carry it along; walk sideways to step out. Both go through portals.
  • Pastes drip from nozzles and fall through portals. Green bounces you, pink makes you fast, and white primer makes a dark panel take portals.
  • A prism crate sends a light beam out of its lens, which points where you were looking when you put it down.
  • Sparks fly straight and bounce. Send one into a socket to open its door. They sting.
  • Presses flatten anything under them as they fall. Wall switches are pressed with GRAB and run on a timer. Slow buttons need weight for a few seconds before they count.
  • Cameras hang on walls. Open a portal on the wall one hangs from and it falls off and goes dark. Bring down every camera in a chamber for the Lens Cap award, and every camera in the game for Blind Spot.
  • Off the test track, cameras sweep a cone of light. Stay in one too long and SaDos puts you back at the start of the section.
  • Light beams burn. A cube on the floor blocks one. Portals carry one from place to place, and a glowing receiver that is lit opens its door.
  • Reset fields close every portal as you pass. Place new ones on the other side.

Tricks

  • Speed: put one portal on the floor and one on the ceiling straight above it, then step into the floor one. You fall through again and again, and the speed meter climbs. The second portal snaps into line with the first to help.
  • Fling: when you are fast, swap the ceiling portal for one on a far wall. You leave it at full speed, and cross gaps no jump could.
  • Portals stay until you fire again or cross a reset field. Fire them while you fall too.
  • Ratings: each chamber has a par time and a par number of portals. Beat both for gold, one for silver. Each chamber also hides one staff badge.
  • Chambers have several rooms. Each door between them is a checkpoint: the arrow button (or R) restarts the room you are in, and Play carries on from it next time.

Reset progress?

Every chamber except the first is locked again, and SaDos forgets she has met you. Options are kept.
