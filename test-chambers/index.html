How to play

The goal

Reach the green EXIT of each chamber. Your portal gun fires two portals, blue and orange, onto white panels only. Step into one and you come out of the other. Speed and direction carry through, so whatever you fall into, you leave at the same speed. Dark panels refuse portals.

Touch · iPad / iPhone

  • Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left half to walk.
  • Right thumb: drag anywhere on the right half to look around.
  • BLUE / ORANGE: fire that portal at the crosshair.
  • JUMP. GRAB: pick up or put down a cube, or tip over a sentry.

Trackpad & keyboard

  • Click once and the pointer locks to the centre, under the gun's sights. Then slide on the trackpad to look around.
  • Two-finger swipe up / down to walk forward / back; sideways to sidestep.
  • Click: blue portal. Two-finger click (or right click): orange.
  • WASD or arrows walk · Space jump · E grab
  • Q blue · X orange · R restart · H this guide · Esc pause · F full screen

Controller

  • Left stick walk · right stick look
  • LT / LB blue · RT / RB orange
  • A jump · X grab · Y restart
  • Start pause · Select full button list

What you will meet

  • Acid: do not touch. SaDos finds this funny.
  • Cubes sit on the round buttons to open doors. Cubes cannot pass a reset field.
  • Sentries see in front of them and shoot after a moment's warning. Break their line of sight, come at them from behind, then GRAB to tip one over. A falling cube also does it.
  • Light beams burn. A cube on the floor blocks one. Portals carry one from place to place, and a glowing receiver that is lit opens its door.
  • Reset fields close every portal as you pass. Place new ones on the other side.

Tricks

  • Speed: put one portal on the floor and one on the ceiling straight above it, then step into the floor one. You fall through again and again, and the speed meter climbs. The second portal snaps into line with the first to help.
  • Fling: when you are fast, swap the ceiling portal for one on a far wall. You leave it at full speed, and cross gaps no jump could.
  • Portals stay until you fire again or cross a reset field. Fire them while you fall too.
  • Stuck? Restart the chamber with the arrow button. Nothing is lost.
