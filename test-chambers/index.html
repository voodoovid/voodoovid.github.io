How to play

Reach the exit of each chamber. Your portal gun fires two portals, blue and orange, onto white panels only. Step into one and you come out of the other. Speed and direction carry through, so what you fall into, you leave at the same speed.

TOUCH (iPad / iPhone)

Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left to walk.
Right thumb: drag to look.
BLUE / ORANGE: fire a portal at the crosshair.
JUMP, and GRAB to pick up or put down a cube.
Fall into a portal from a height to build speed.

KEYBOARD AND MOUSE

WASD / arrows: move · Mouse: look
Left click: blue · Right click: orange
Space: jump · E: grab / drop
R: restart chamber · Esc: pause · F: full screen

CONTROLLER

Sticks move and look · LT / LB blue · RT / RB orange
A jump · X grab · Start pause · Select shows the full list

Cubes can sit on the round floor buttons to open doors. Acid is bad. SaDos has opinions about this.
